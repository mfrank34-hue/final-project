## Fall 2026 DTSC 2302 Group Project
## Project Overview 

This project investigates whether historical weather
patterns can help predict the next day's maximum
temperature in Charlotte, North Carolina.

We will examine temperature, rainfall, and seasonal
patterns to identify relationships that may improve
the accuracy of temperature predictions.
